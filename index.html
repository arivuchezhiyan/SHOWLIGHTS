Who We Are

Crafting Light
into Art Since 2009

Showlites was born from a singular vision — to transform interior spaces into luminous masterpieces. Since 2009, we have been at the forefront of premium chandelier design, combining centuries-old craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics to create lighting that transcends the ordinary.

Every Showlites piece is a bespoke creation, meticulously handcrafted from the finest materials — precision-cut crystals, French Gold metalwork, Italian marble, and premium hand-blown glass. Our design consultants work closely with homeowners, architects, and interior designers across India to deliver lighting installations that are as unique as the spaces they illuminate.

From intimate bedroom pendants to grand hotel lobby chandeliers, from elegant double-height statement pieces to functional fandeliers, Showlites delivers premium lighting across every room type — backed by our commitment to quality, timely installation, and dedicated after-sales care.
